1. Introduction

This provisional introduction outlines the structure and workflow of the paper. Scientific concepts, propulsion architectures, and habitat design requirements for interstellar generation ships have been extensively explored in the literature [1, 2].

1.1 Scope and Objectives

Each section of this manuscript lives in its own dedicated directory containing its LaTeX source, figures (images/), and tables (tables/). Cross-references such as section 2 and table 1 update automatically whenever sections, figures, tables, or citations are added or reordered.
